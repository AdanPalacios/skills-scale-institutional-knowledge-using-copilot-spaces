# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's project management guidance. Together, these documents describe how cross-functional work moves from an idea to delivery and ongoing improvement.

## Process overview

OctoAcme begins by validating an initiative's business need, aligning stakeholders, defining measurable success criteria, and deciding whether it is ready for planning. The team then turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified dependencies and risks.

During execution, teams deliver iteratively and track work on a project board, using regular team check-ins, delivery reviews, and demos to surface progress and blockers. The PM coordinates schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager defines outcomes and prioritization; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance and quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and communication are managed throughout the lifecycle through regular status updates, risk reviews, and escalation paths.

Before release, OctoAcme checks acceptance criteria, CI and security results, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans. After deployment, the team verifies and communicates the release. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture lessons and turn them into a small set of owned, trackable improvement actions.
